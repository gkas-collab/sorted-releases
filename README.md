# Sorted

Sorted is a Mac email app for Microsoft 365 mailboxes. This page holds the downloads.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/gkas-collab/sorted-releases/releases/latest) and download `Sorted.zip`.
2. Double-click the zip, then drag **Sorted** into your Applications folder.
3. Open Sorted. The first time, macOS will say it cannot check the app. Close that message, open
   **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
4. Click **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your work account.

## Updates

Sorted checks this page for new versions by itself. When one is ready it shows an "Update and reopen" button.

Your mail goes directly between your Mac and Microsoft. Rules, filters and settings are kept on your own Mac.
